Click a colony to light it up. Everything else stays grey.

About this map. Outlines are modern state borders, the way most textbook maps draw them. Maine is shown as part of Massachusetts (it was, until 1820) and present-day West Virginia is inside Virginia. The dashed area between New York and New Hampshire is the New Hampshire Grants — later Vermont — claimed by both and not one of the thirteen. Founding dates are the ones textbooks use; several colonies changed hands or charters more than once.
